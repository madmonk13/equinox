    An homage to the light-versus-dark strategy duels of the 1980s.

    How to play

    Goal

    Occupy all five power points (glowing rune squares) at once, or destroy every enemy piece.

    Turns

    Click one of your pieces, then a highlighted square. Ground pieces step orthogonally and can't pass other pieces. Flying and teleporting pieces go anywhere in range.

    Battles

    Moving onto an enemy starts a real-time duel in the arena. The loser is removed; the winner keeps its wounds. Wounds heal slowly, and instantly on power points.

    Luminance

    Some squares are always light or dark; the shimmering ones cycle each round. Fighting on a square of your color grants bonus hit points.

    Spells

    Your spellcaster (Archmage or Necromancer) can cast each of seven spells once per game instead of moving. Pieces on power points are immune to spells.

    Arena controls

    WASD/Arrows move · Space attack in the direction you face. In two-player mode Light uses WASD + Space, Dark uses Arrows + Enter.

    Saving

    Your game saves at the end of every turn. Reloading resumes from the start of the current turn.
